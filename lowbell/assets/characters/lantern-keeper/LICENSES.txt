The current OLLY OLLY public/ASSET-LICENSES.md records that the Jack pipeline
resamples the Renderpeople skeleton and locomotion, transfers skin weights,
and builds its straw, clothing and pumpkin geometry. Locomotion was retained
from the Winterline Mixamo / Three.js Soldier conversion, then hunched and
resampled. Additional creature actions were authored for OLLY OLLY.
Do not distribute the embedded animation clips as a standalone library.

LOWBELL changes: original asset bytes retained. Runtime uses JackBody and the
shriek carving JackHeadB, the original compressed atlas, muted cloth/rind
grading, physical bone reactions and captured-pose ragdoll. Overhead lantern
throw and hand attachment are new procedural poses on those actual joints.
