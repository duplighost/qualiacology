a character foundation, rig, eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes, tongue, teeth, and skin
created using MPFB 2.0.17 and MakeHuman system assets. The MakeHuman system

- MakeHuman system assets: <https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html>

MPFB 2.0.17 is GPL-3.0-or-later. MPFB and Blender are authoring tools and are
not redistributed in the playable browser release. The Blender character
authoring scripts included in the source release are GPL-3.0-or-later.

### `culturalibre_hair_17` / “Female hairs”

The character model includes “Female hairs,” originally created by miccall and
adapted for MakeHuman by culturalibre. It is distributed in the MakeHuman
Community Hair 03 asset pack under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0
International License:

- Original model: <https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/female-hairs-0a391b6508a241f4b0b399f403ee4602>
- MakeHuman adaptation: <http://www.makehumancommunity.org/node/2481>
- Hair 03 asset pack: <https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/hair03.html>
- CC BY 4.0: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>

Project changes: the hair material is tinted deep crimson, its roughness is
adjusted, and the object is widened by 1.08 on X and 1.06 on Y before being
combined with the character. No endorsement by miccall, culturalibre,
Sketchfab, or the MakeHuman Community is implied.

### `culturalibre_hero-heroine_gloves_4`

The character model includes gloves created by culturalibre and distributed in
the MakeHuman Community Gloves 01 asset pack under CC0 1.0:

- Gloves 01 asset pack: <https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/gloves01.html>
- CC0 1.0: <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>

Project changes: the donor diffuse treatment is replaced by a pearl-silver
metallic material, the live subdivision modifier is removed for the game GLB,
and original segmented gauntlet plates are layered over the gloves.

No third-party environment, animation, effect, music, or sound assets are
included in this project.
