GORETHREAD // HELLSPINDLE

Open this file:
  index.html

Keep index.html, style.css, game.js, and the assets folder together.

The Desktop zip's one-file PLAY html is the old flat geometry. This folder is the painted one.

Desktop: A/D move, Space jump, S drops a ledge or a grate, hold mouse for the
wheel, Tab opens the Reliquary.
Phone: landscape. Left thumb moves (swipe up jump, swipe down drop). Right thumb
is the wheel. Tap the badge when a relic is waiting.

Twelve districts, four heights each: the vault, the gallery, the road, and the
undercroft under the grate. Kill things to level, then spend the relic yourself.
Pause shows where you are.
Click continues a save. N on the title is a new descent.
