01 · Psychopharmacology

The Psychopharmacology Server

Convergence-based reasoning around MAOIs, serotonin toxicity, and the difference between what works on paper and what works in people.

Enter the field notes
“Truth in psychiatric medicine doesn’t emerge from any single source. It emerges when multiple ways of knowing converge.”

Mechanism, trials, expert judgment, prescriber observation, and patient experience belong at the same table. The community exists to compare notes without treating anecdotes like scripture.
