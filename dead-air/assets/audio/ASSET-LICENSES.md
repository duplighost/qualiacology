# DEAD AIR audio sources

The firearm and footstep acquisition record below comes from Alex's WINTERLINE library. DEAD AIR includes the carbine and shotgun excerpts and four concrete steps. The original preparation-script references describe that library.

# Firearm recordings

The runtime WAV files are excerpts prepared directly from **The Free Firearm Sound Library**, created and recorded by **Ben Jaszczak, Brian Nelson, Kevin Heras, and Matthew Nanney**.

License: **CC0 1.0 Universal**. The collection permits personal and professional use without royalty or credit.

- Original collection and license: https://opengameart.org/content/the-free-firearm-sound-library
- Original prepared archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/Prepared%20SFX%20Library.7z
- CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The archive's original `Prepared Master Sheet.csv` identifies these recordings:

| Runtime files | Original recording | Microphone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| pistol-report-* | Walther PPQ / X_39P.wav | Near |
| pistol-tail-* | Walther PPQ / X_31P.wav | Mid distance |
| carbine-report-* | AK-47 / C_28P.wav | Near |
| carbine-tail-* | AK-47 / C_31P.wav | Mid distance |
| shotgun-report-* | Benelli Nova / O_21P.wav | Near |
| shotgun-tail-* | Benelli Nova / O_17P.wav | Mid distance |

Modifications: genuine separate takes isolated, converted to 48 kHz mono, DC offset removed, level adjusted, short click-prevention fades applied; direct attack omitted from field-tail files. These are sound-design layers for fictional game weapons, not a claim of exact acoustic matching to their visual models. The preparation script and exact onset positions are retained in the development `work/audio-sources` folder.

## Footsteps

`step-0.wav` through `step-3.wav` are isolated steps from **Footsteps, Shoe on Concrete (0514)** by **Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank**, released under **CC0** by the creator.

- Original source and creator license declaration: https://bigsoundbank.com/footsteps-shoe-on-concrete-s0514.html
- Creator's license terms: https://bigsoundbank.com/licenses.html
- Download used: https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/mp3/0514.mp3

Modifications: four distinct steps trimmed from the original recording, level adjusted and faded; mono 48 kHz PCM WAV. The exact cuts and preparation script are retained under `work/audio-sources`.

## Coastal ambience and foley

`sea.ogg`: Sea waves (0698), Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank, CC0. https://bigsoundbank.com/sea-waves-s0698.html . A 36-second excerpt starting at 22 seconds, faded and converted to Ogg Vorbis.

`metal_hit_01/02/03.ogg`, `metal_close_01.ogg`, `lock_open_01.ogg`, `metal_slam_01.ogg`, `metal_falling_01.ogg`, `wood_hit_01.ogg`: rubberduck, 100 CC0 metal and wood SFX, CC0. https://opengameart.org/content/100-cc0-metal-and-wood-sfx . Runtime playback applies pitch, distance attenuation, low-pass filtering and room reverb.
