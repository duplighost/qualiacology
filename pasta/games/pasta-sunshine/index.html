Touch: left thumb moves; drag the open screen to look and aim. Buttons adapt to the mission: GRAB, TALK, DIVE or DOWN. On Pinna’s coaster, aim at Mecha-Bowser and press SPRAY with a rocket loaded. The sand castle, cannon and hotel secrets remove the sprayer. Pachinko and Lily Pad keep it. Use DIVE on Pinna’s timed red paths and in Pianta’s race; jump then DOWN to break the hotel’s watermelon block. At Delfino Airstrip, collect Turbo, jump then DOWN on the red switch, and hold BOOST to race the coins. In Corona, spray backward to propel the boat; charge Rocket, release, then press DOWN near the jump’s peak. In Delfino Plaza, press Tab / JOURNAL to track any of eleven objectives. Collect the Rocket and Turbo boxes near the fountain. G talks, carries fruit, mounts Yoshi, enters sewers, or pays the Airstrip captain. Spray a Rainbow M, then jump through it. Jump into pipes and the cannon; walk into the Corona cave. At Noki Bay’s marker, use V / LOOK and look straight up. The Sirena roof pipe opens when mounted Yoshi eats its pineapple. The Airstrip ferry costs ten yellow coins. All nineteen blue coins save immediately; exchange ten at the boathouse. Pachinko: LAUNCH, steer left/right, hold JUMP with Hover selected, then RETURN from a pocket. Lily Pad: feed and ride Yoshi, cross the ferries, juice the orange pipe seal, then steer the raft by spraying backward. In Noki Bay, hold GRAB to carry a sprayed bomb or a springboard. In Pianta Village, hold PULL and walk backward to stretch a cooled Chomplet’s tail, then release toward the pool.

ABOUT THIS BUILD

Every strand counts.

Fifteen playable destinations from Super Mario Sunshine, with thirty mission Shines and one currently available blue-coin exchange. The opening title, scenery, characters, enemies and effects use modeled pasta.

Delfino Plaza contains all eleven town Shine objectives in one visit, nineteen persistent blue coins, fruit deliveries, Yoshi, the nozzle boxes, a sewer network and the boathouse trader. Its five secret courses are Pachinko, Lily Pad Ride, Super Slide, Turbo Track and Red Coin Field. The journal tracks your discoveries and marks entrances on the map. It never moves you to a different world.

Every game starts in Delfino Plaza. Reach a world through its painting, pipe, cannon, sun marker, cave or ferry. Episode choices appear at that entrance, and leaving a level returns you to the town. All seven main worlds, Delfino Airstrip and Corona Mountain remain available; original story-based entrance locks are not applied to this smaller mission set. Every Shine has its own medal, best time and coin record on this device.

These are approximations of the original maps and mechanics, with physically based pasta shading and selected strand simulations. They are not measured one-to-one reconstructions or full physical simulations of every strand.
