WATERFALL IMAGE GENERATION PROMPT
Create a photographic texture asset for a realistic 3D nature video game: a single broad naturally irregular curtain of falling waterfall water, front-on, isolated against a fully transparent background. Vertical rectangular composition. The sheet is approximately 7 meters tall and 4 meters wide in implied scale. Water starts along a gently uneven horizontal lip at the very top without any visible rock. Thin silvery transparent streamlets gather into multiple heavier white cascades, with darker transparent gaps, subtle fine droplets, wisps of mist, and natural turbulent variation. The water accelerates downward and fans out slightly at the bottom into soft loose spray, no pool shown. Real long-exposure outdoor waterfall photograph at a modest shutter speed, detailed distinct streaks and flowing translucent water, beautiful convincing real fluid detail. Mostly soft translucent silver grey and white water, preserve true alpha transparency through the water and empty gaps; no uniformly opaque white rectangle. No rocks, no trees, no ground, no pool, no landscape, no sky, no background color, no borders, no words. Straight front view with no perspective so the alpha texture can wrap onto a curved 3D ribbon. The edges of the waterfall must be irregular and feather into individual narrow falling streams.
