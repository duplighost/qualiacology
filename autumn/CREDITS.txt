AUTUMN - Alexander Duncan Guitar / Qualiacology

Forest generation, procedural tree shapes, landscape, arrangements, animation,
waterways, and sound synthesis created for this game.

Autumn canopy and fallen maple imagery generated with OpenAI's built-in image
generation tool for this game. Runtime files: textures/autumn-canopy-v1.png and
textures/fallen-maple-v1.png. The original generated alpha is preserved.
Water, falls, spray, animal geometry and movement are rendered in real time.
