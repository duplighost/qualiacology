# Collectibles

The six models (`gold-statuette`, `platinum-record`, `diamond-earring`,
`gold-pocket-watch`, `crystal-award`, `magician-box`) and their renders were
made for STARSTRUCK in GPT-6 Pro's optional support collection: original
geometry and programmatic surface graphics, no third-party assets. The `.webp`
thumbnails are cropped and resized from that collection's renders.
