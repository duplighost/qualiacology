## Police helicopter (`police-helicopter.glb`) — CC0 1.0

"Light helicopter" by Innerscene, from the Innerscene Free Parts Library
(via GPT-6 Pro's "Beyond the Gates" pack, `models/innerscene/light-helicopter.glb`).
Source: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/3d-parts/light-helicopter-eab26245
Terms: https://www.innerscene.com/tools/library/terms

Modified for STARSTRUCK: rotor blades and the cabin interior removed (the game
draws spinning rotor discs instead), simplified to about 35% of its vertices,
textures resized to 512 px WebP, meshopt-compressed (`gltf-transform optimize`).
The game repaints it black and white and makes the glazing opaque.

CC0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
