# Furniture and props

Chosen from GPT-6 Pro's "Rich Life" support pack, then simplified, given WebP
textures and meshopt-compressed for the game (`gltf-transform optimize`).

## Concept car (`car.glb`) — CC BY 4.0

Car Concept by Eric Chadwick / Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH (2024), based on a
public-domain concept model by Unity Fan.
Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Modified: logo textures and logo-bearing tire sidewall maps removed, blank
license plate, default red materials kept, alternate material variants removed
(by the support pack); simplified to about 15% of its vertices, textures
resized to 1024 px WebP and meshopt-compressed (for STARSTRUCK).

## Telescope (`telescope.glb`)

The brass refractor on its walnut tripod in the observatory's west wing. Geometry generated by
GPT-6 Pro for Alex in the "Optional Extras Vol04" delivery (its LICENSES.md: supplied for reuse
and modification in his projects, without an additional restriction); meshopt-compressed and
its textures made WebP for the game.

## Portraits (`portraits/`) — CC0 (The Met Open Access)

Two John Singer Sargent paintings on the villa's great-hall walls, from The Metropolitan Museum
of Art's Open Access images (public domain, CC0): `madame-x.webp` (Madame X, 1883–84, object
16.53) and `marquand.webp` (Henry G. Marquand, 1897, object 97.43). Resized to 1024 px tall.
Policy: https://www.metmuseum.org/hubs/open-access
