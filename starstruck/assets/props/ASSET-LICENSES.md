# Furniture and props

Chosen from GPT-6 Pro's "Rich Life" support pack, then simplified, given WebP
textures and meshopt-compressed for the game (`gltf-transform optimize`).

## Concept car (`car.glb`) — CC BY 4.0

Car Concept by Eric Chadwick / Darmstadt Graphics Group GmbH (2024), based on a
public-domain concept model by Unity Fan.
Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/CarConcept
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Modified: logo textures and logo-bearing tire sidewall maps removed, blank
license plate, default red materials kept, alternate material variants removed
(by the support pack); simplified to about 15% of its vertices, textures
resized to 1024 px WebP and meshopt-compressed (for STARSTRUCK).
