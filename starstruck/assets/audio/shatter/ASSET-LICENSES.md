# Breaking things

`impactPlate_*`, `impactGlass_*`, `impactMining_*`, `impactMetal_*` and
`impactBell_*` are from **Impact Sounds** (1.0) by **Kenney** (www.kenney.nl),
unchanged, copied from DEAD AIR's work folder (D:\Projects\dead-air\work\kenney-impact).
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
License: Creative Commons Zero, CC0 1.0 — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The game layers and pitches them by material (porcelain, crystal, glass,
stone, metal) in `src/world/shatter-audio.js`; the bulb pop, the crystal
shimmer and the splash are synthesized there at boot.
