# Character provenance for THE SENTENCE — IRON RIOT

## Included derivative

The game uses `assets/eric.glb` and `assets/clothing-mask.png`, recovered from the user's supplied website source archive. The source archive recorded the acquisition and conversion lineage below. This build did not independently reacquire the original FBX or perform the earlier animation retargeting.

The newly written game clones the skeleton, blends its Idle/Walk/Run clips, recolors clothing through the mask, adds simple prisoner/guard/warden equipment, and implements new collapse posing and ballistic corpse-root motion. It does not use another game's rifle asset, controller, level, or game code.

The source archive's provenance records the license as CC BY 4.0 and records acquisition from the creator's official free FBX pack. No representation is made that the creator endorsed this game.

The derivative has an 88-bone bound skeleton and approximately 20,539 triangles. The earlier conversion record states: FBX-to-glTF conversion, duplicate vertex merging, normalized skinning influences, root scaling, and embedded optimized diffuse/normal/roughness textures. The clothing mask derives from the creator's suit/trouser/shirt masks packed into red/green channels. Source facial and skin detail is retained.

This game adds orange and navy palettes, belts, patches, caps and shields; it does not claim a newly scanned or bespoke character cast.
