STORMGLASS Enforcer, the city's corp-sec gunman (enforcer.glb) - provenance

Body and skeleton
  Quaternius "Universal Base Characters [Standard]" - Superhero_Male_FullBody.gltf
  CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
  https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html (downloaded from quaternius.itch.io)
  Changes: rest pose re-proportioned (broader chest and shoulders, longer arms); head and feet removed under
  the helmet and boots; decimated; the skin texture replaced by baked materials; scaled to 1.85 m.

Animation
  Quaternius "Universal Animation Library [Standard]" and "Universal Animation Library 2 [Standard]"
  CC0 1.0. https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html and
  https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary2.html
  Clip bodies used: Pistol_Idle_Loop -> Idle (and the stance and breath under Aim and Fire),
  Walk_Loop -> Walk, Jog_Fwd_Loop -> Run, Hit_Chest -> Hit, Idle_Shield_Break -> Stagger, Death01 -> Death.
  Pistol_Idle_Loop and Punch_Jab lent finger poses. On every frame both arms are re-solved by two-bone IK so
  the hands stay on the rifle; spine, head and shoulder corrections and a boots-on-the-street pass are layered
  on top. Location keys rescaled with the model.
  Aim, Fire, StrafeL, StrafeR and Melee are original, keyed by script on the same rig.

Armour, helmet and visor, boots, pouches, power pack, the bullpup rifle, materials and baked textures

  anim_enforcer.py, city_bake.py, city_meta.py, run_city.sh), with the thaw pipeline's ground_offsets.py.
  No third-party meshes or images.
