STORMGLASS heroine asset provenance

Candidate file: heroine-quaternius-candidate.glb

Adapted from Alex's original Nera heroine in Secondhand Saint:
C:/Users/Alex/Projects/secondhand-saint/assets/characters/nera-player-v016.glb
Source SHA-256: a780b786862ede1e1e1bb08bc8fc6a3069632e0014daa529b9fcf893eea87d08
The donor original was read and has not been modified.
Complete anatomical body recovered from the editable source:
C:/Users/Alex/Projects/secondhand-saint/work/character-pipeline/nera-shell-v016-final/nera-shell-v016.blend
The original glove mask was removed in the derivative only, and the stored
body shape/rest correction was baked into the new mesh.

The body, rig, eyes, eyebrows, eyelashes, teeth, tongue and skin foundation
were authored using MPFB 2.0.17 and MakeHuman system assets, distributed CC0.
https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/makehuman_system_assets.html
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
MPFB and Blender are authoring tools, not included in the browser game.

The earlier culturalibre_hero-heroine_gloves_4 glove mesh has been removed
from this derivative; hands and forearms now use the complete body surface.

Hair: cortu_strawberry_cloud_hair by Cortu Johnstone, CC0.
https://static.makehumancommunity.org/assets/assetpacks/hair01.html
Official asset-pack page verified on 2026-10-21; names this exact asset CC0.
The source .mhclo and .mhmat also identify Cortu Johnstone and CC0.
Source files: D:/Tools/MakeHuman-CC0-hair01/cortu_strawberry_cloud_hair/
Adaptations: fitted/reoriented geometry, auburn shader tint, head skinning,
adjusted normal intensity and roughness. Original source files are untouched.

Nera's earlier culturalibre_hair_17 CC-BY hair and original long hair-card layer
were removed from this exported derivative; they are not included here.

The earlier hard armor, pauldrons, cloak, and thin straps have been removed.
STORMGLASS adds an original fitted ivory halter garment and boots from the
CC0 body surface, proportional adjustments, a named sword attachment, and
five preserved original clips (Run, Dodge, Heavy, Hit, Death) and five
retargeted Quaternius clips (Idle, Guard, Slash1, Slash2, Slash3).
Anatomical rest-roll alignment, proportional pelvis translation, explicit
recovery timing, continuous quaternion signs, and foot correction are baked.
Idle/Guard use independent analytical knee/foot grounding.

No commercial-game characters, textures, animation clips, or trademarks were
extracted or copied. No endorsement by the original asset creators is implied.
