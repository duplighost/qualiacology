SIDECHAIN

Privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy covers SIDECHAIN, the game formerly called LAST CHORUS, on qualiacology.com and in its Android app. It is provided by Alexander Duncan Guitar, under the name Qualiacology.

SIDECHAIN has no player accounts, ads, or in-app purchases. Your scores, unlocks, and settings are saved on your device. The game needs an internet connection to load its files and music.

Progress and settings on your device

The game uses your browser's local storage for scores, grades, play counts, experience, unlocked riders and boards, your selected equipment, and performance settings. SIDECHAIN does not upload this saved progress or sync it to a server.

The Android app opens the same game through a supported browser. Its saved progress can therefore be shared with visits to the game in that browser on the same device.

This data stays on your device until you clear the website's storage or your browser removes it. To erase it, use your browser's site settings to delete stored data for qualiacology.com. This can also clear saved data for other games on the same site. Uninstalling the Android app alone may leave data in the browser.

Loading the game and website logs

The website is hosted by Netlify. When the game requests a page, song, image, or other file, the hosting service receives normal connection information: your IP address, the requested address, the time of the request, browser and device information sent by the browser, and a referring address if one is sent. Hosting tools may also derive an approximate region from the IP address and record response status, timing, and bandwidth.

This information is used to deliver the game, protect the service from abuse, understand website traffic, and diagnose loading or performance problems. It is separate from your locally saved scores and unlocks. SIDECHAIN does not include advertising trackers or a separate gameplay analytics service.

Hosting logs are retained and expire according to Netlify's service settings and retention schedules. The game does not maintain its own remote player database. More information is available in Netlify's hosting-log documentation and Netlify's privacy statement.

Support messages

If you email alexdguitar@gmail.com, your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send are used to respond to your request. They are handled through the email provider and retained in the support mailbox for the request and related follow-up. You can ask for those messages to be deleted.

Service providers and security

Netlify processes website requests to provide hosting. The email provider processes messages you choose to send for support. Information may also need to be disclosed where required by law. SIDECHAIN does not sell player information or provide it to advertising networks.

Game files are delivered over HTTPS. Your device, browser, and Google Play may process information under their own settings and policies. This policy describes SIDECHAIN's handling of information; it does not replace those services' policies. See Google's privacy policy for Google services.

Your choices and deletion requests

You can delete local progress through the browser's site settings as described above. There is no SIDECHAIN account to delete. For questions about information associated with a website request or support message, or to request access, correction, or deletion, email alexdguitar@gmail.com.

Requests concerning hosting records depend on what records still exist and what the hosting provider can identify or delete. Some records may need to be retained for security or legal obligations. Do not send passwords, payment details, or identity documents with a privacy request.

Children

SIDECHAIN contains mature song content and is not directed at children. It does not ask players to provide their name, age, email address, or other profile information. If you believe a child has sent personal information through a support message, contact the address above.

Changes to this policy

Changes to the game's data handling will be reflected on this page, with an updated date. Material changes that require notice or consent will be explained before the new handling begins.
