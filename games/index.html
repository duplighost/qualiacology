  • Hoverboard ride through thirty-six songs

    SIDECHAIN

    Thirty-six songs. One board. Every dip is a downbeat, every hill a bar — pump in time and the record carries you: through a car crash, a candy factory at night, a library where every scream has a surname. Ride it to the last chorus. Keyboard, mouse or thumbs.

    • An hour and a half or so
    Ride

  • Halloween first-person horror

    OLLY OLLY

    One Halloween night on Alder Lane. Twelve houses, a churchyard and a forest, scarecrows that get up off the lawns, trick-or-treaters in rubber masks, and something tall in a bedsheet looking for you. Keyboard and mouse.

    Go out

  • open-world night horror

    We Were Promised Morning

    One county of forest and back roads, and the sun does not come back. Walk or drive between the last lit places, shoot what comes out of the trees, and take a little of the map back every time you get somewhere.

    Go outside

  • Two courses made of pasta

    SPAGHETTI SUNSHINE

    The first Shine in Bianco Hills and Ricco Harbor. All spaghetti. Even Mario. Cross the village, clear the road to the windmill, then climb through the harbor and pull Gooper Blooper out of the crates. Both courses in the same game, with a Shine saved for each. Keyboard and mouse.

    • Two first Shines
    Play

  • 3D platformer made of pasta

    AL DENTE

    It must all be made of absolutely photorealistic pasta and behave that way too. You are a forkful of cooked spaghetti. Cooked pasta sticks to cooked pasta, so you can climb the lasagna. Dry spaghetti snaps under you. Cavatappi are springs, the tagliatelle bridge sags, and the star is in a nest at the top of a rigatoni. Keyboard and mouse.

    • A few minutes
    Play AL DENTE

  • Side-scroller made of spaghetti

    SPAGHETTI

    Everything is made out of spaghetti. Nothing cannot be spaghetti. You are a twirl of it, walking on two loose ends, in five levels: on the table, under a ceiling of it, high up on bundles of it, in a fortress of it at dusk with a knot at the end, and at night among the racks, swinging from it. Dry spaghetti snaps. Cooked spaghetti stretches until it snaps too. Keyboard.

    • Twenty minutes or so
    Jump in

  • A fledgling on your real desktop

    SILL

    A window opens on your desktop and something falls out of it. It can’t fly yet. It stands on your real windows and rides them when you move them. Get it home, feed it, keep it away from the cat, and then let it go. A Windows download, not a page.

    • Fifteen minutes or so
    Open the window

  • First-person catch-and-throw

    COLLATERAL

    Take it. Throw it back. Hold to catch their fire while the world slows, and the gauntlet tears up whatever you look at. Let go and all of it goes back at them. Five districts, one above the next, and the only way up is through the floor. Keyboard and mouse.

    Take it

  • Endless walk through all four seasons

    IN EVERY DIRECTION

    First person. An autumn forest that goes on forever in every direction, with crunching leaves, piles of leaves and falling leaves. A swing in a huge old tree, a cabin with smoke coming out of the chimney, a bench someone put where the view is, and so many more that it’s impossible to find them all. Walk north and it slowly turns to winter, west into spring, south into summer, and you can get lost in any of them forever. The sun rises in the east and sets in the west, and at night the sky is filled with stars and different animals come out. You can hunt animals with a rifle and catch fish with a fishing rod. No HUD. Keyboard and mouse.

    • As long as you like
    Walk in

  • A salmon swims home up a river

    THE WAY I CAME

    A salmon, from the sea at dawn to the gravel where it was born. The water is simulated: it pours over the weirs, curls behind the rocks and races through the gorge. Rest in the slow water, find a way through the fast, and leap from the white water under each step. The others swim with you. Not all of them make it. Keyboard and mouse, or two thumbs on a phone held sideways.

    • Forty minutes or so
    Swim

  • Top-down arcade

    HEARSE

    One dark church, seven candles, and a spinning light on a chain. Hold to spin it, let go to throw it, and it comes back. Every hit puts a candle out. A hearse was a candle stand before it was a car. Keyboard and mouse, or two thumbs on a phone held sideways.

    Play HEARSE

  • Round-the-world stone skip

    SKIP

    Skip a stone off a beach at dusk. Click or tap as it touches the water. If you keep going it travels all the way around the world: the bay, a creek, a town, a church, the ice, the open sea, and back to the beach by morning. Mouse, or one thumb on a phone.

    Play SKIP

  • Echolocation night flight

    BELFRY

    A bat on Halloween night. It’s dark, and you only see what makes a sound. The colony left for the winter roost while you were asleep, and they’re hunting all over town on the way. Find them. Every one you find clicks too, so the more of you there are, the more you can see. Keyboard and mouse, or two thumbs on a phone held sideways.

    • Ten minutes or so
    Let go

  • Musical high-wire walk

    STRUNG

    A tightrope across a city at night, eight ropes from roof to roof to the clock tower. Every rope is a string and your feet play it. Lean and it goes out of tune. There’s a net until there isn’t. Mouse, or two thumbs on a phone.

    Step out

  • Throw-the-road skate game

    ARC

    There's no road until you throw one. You carry a swift whose tail is made of light: throw it and the tail hangs in the air as a rail. Ride the rail. It ends in mid-air over black water, so call the swift back — it eats the road on the way — and catching it at speed throws you into the next throw. Three big slow animals in the way, and a dam at the end.

    • Twenty minutes or so

  • Third-person action

    Tideglass

    A chained glass weight, a flooded glassworks, and a way out to the sea. Draw the weight along the ground or stand it up, turn the capstans until the water drops, and break whatever the sea engine is hiding behind.

    Play Tideglass

  • First-person survival shooter

    STARSTRUCK

    A survival game with an addictive level-up system. One night in the Hills: mansions, a studio backlot, a cemetery, a waterfall you can walk through, the dead always coming, and bosses that come at certain intervals, in certain places. A gun in the right hand, a throwing star in the left, XP that soars into you like a magnet, and a skill tree in the sky that keeps what you've placed. How long can you survive. Keyboard and mouse.

    • As long as you last
    Survive the night
