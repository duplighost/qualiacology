  • Round-moon horde arcade

    BOON MOOTS

    A pocket moon, and you are standing on it — walk far enough in any direction and you arrive behind yourself. One ball, kicked at whatever is coming, over one horizon and back over the other. A troll walks at you announcing that the task is complete and its confidence is high. Milo made this one, for me.

    Stand on it

  • One-bird murmuration

    STARLING

    You are one bird in three thousand. You cannot tell the flock anything — you can only turn, and the birds beside you turn a beat later, and theirs a beat after that. The wave of it crosses the whole murmuration faster than any bird can fly. The falcon hunts the calmest part of it, so turning early is the same as not turning at all.

    Fly with them

  • One-rope dog walk

    LEAD

    The dog gets faster the less you hold it. Hold on and it can only go as fast as your own legs. Open your hand and it runs. Nothing here can be hurt — opening your hand is still the hard part.

    Hold on

    THROWN
