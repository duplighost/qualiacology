src/assets/audio/CREDITS.txt

Little Revolutions — original instrumental composition for TINBOUND, 2026.
Composed and arranged in tools/compose-score.py; 96 bars at 136 BPM.
Instrumentation: sampled marimba, clarinet, pizzicato strings, upright bass,
nylon guitar, vibraphone and brush drums. No existing melody or MIDI is reused.

Instrument samples: GeneralUser GS 2.0.3 by S. Christian Collins.
The author's license permits private and commercial musical recordings.
Read GeneralUser-LICENSE.txt for the complete license and provenance notes.
https://www.schristiancollins.com/generaluser
https://github.com/mrbumpy409/GeneralUser-GS

Rendered offline with FluidSynth 2.6.2; encoded with FFmpeg/libmp3lame.
https://github.com/FluidSynth/fluidsynth/releases/tag/v2.6.2
The game needs only score.mp3; the SoundFont and renderer are build tools.

Loose Ends and Night Freight — original instrumental compositions for TINBOUND,2026.
Composed and arranged in tools/compose-interiors.py.
Loose Ends: 80 bars of 3/4 at114BPM; guitar, flute, piano, pizzicato and vibraphone.
Night Freight: 80 bars of 4/4 at144BPM; marimba, bassoon, accordion, bass, pizzicato and brushes.
The same GeneralUser GS2.0.3 license and rendering credits above apply.
The three MP3 arrangements are embedded in the offline game.

The little bird's whistle phrases, repair timing and synthesis are original
TINBOUND sound design in src/audio.js and src/bird-voice.js. Repair strokes and thread movement also
layer the credited Kenney CC0 impacts and cloth-like filtered noise. No voice,
bird-song recording or external melody is sampled by these synthesized cues.
