Doopliss

Bright, catchy, upbeat melodies with psychologically dark, emotionally raw lyrics — murder pop on purpose. The hooks invite you in. The subtext quietly locks the door behind you.

The artist

Doopliss

Doopliss lives in that deliciously wrong sweet spot between neon-pop seduction and emotional sabotage — playful, glossy, a little dangerous, and never interested in pretending the dark stuff only sounds dark.
